02 / OPERATING MODEL

I turn complex boundaries into systems teams can trust.

  1. 01

    Read the real boundary

    Trace device state, packets, protocols, failure modes, and the people operating the product before choosing a framework.

  2. 02

    Form an executable contract

    Turn the complexity into an API, SDK, gateway, emulator, or source of truth that can be executed—not just described.

  3. 03

    Verify before trust

    Separate contract, unit, DOM, UI/CSS, E2E, and evaluation gates so generated code and automation can fail visibly.

  4. 04

    Leave a reusable method

    Package decisions into workflows, examples, review rules, and mentoring so another person can apply and verify the method.
