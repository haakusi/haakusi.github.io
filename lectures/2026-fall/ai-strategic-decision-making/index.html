A six-week journey from framing strategic problems and using generative AI responsibly to tool choice, human-AI collaboration, verification, metacognition, algorithmic limits, ethics, and a personal strategy roadmap.

Problem framing → AI-assisted exploration → comparison → verification & ethics → human decision → feedback

W2Chapters 4–5

Reliability and AI Intervention Points

Hallucination review, evidence checks, and a staged structure for inserting AI into strategic decisions.
W3Chapters 6–8

Tools, Collaboration, and Prompt Design

Choosing decision tools, designing human-led collaboration, and prompting with explicit criteria and branches.
W4Chapters 9–10

Interpretation, Verification, and Metacognition

Turning outputs into strategy through technical, contextual, and operational verification—and checking one's own assumptions.
W5Chapters 11–12

Problem Redefinition and Algorithmic Limits

Separating symptoms from causes, using 5 Whys and Fishbone, and selecting algorithms with their failure modes in view.
W6Chapters 13–14

Ethics and the Future Strategist

Embedding fairness, transparency, accountability, privacy, and reliability into a five-year personal strategy.
