4장. 할루시네이션과 신뢰성 검토

할루시네이션은 AI가 현실에 없는 정보를 그럴듯하게 생성하는 현상이다. 언어모델은 사실 데이터베이스를 그대로 조회하기보다 다음 토큰의 가능성을 계산하므로, 유창성과 진실성은 분리된다. 이는 예외적 결함이라기보다 생성 원리에서 계속 관리해야 할 위험이다.

오류는 사실·인용·논리·시점·편향·맥락 누락·요약 왜곡·과도한 단정·정보 누락과 중복 등으로 나타난다. 신뢰성 검토는 다음 다섯 질문을 고정한다.

  1. 출처가 실제로 존재하고 원문과 일치하는가?
  2. 주장과 결론 사이의 논리 구조가 타당한가?
  3. 자료의 시점과 적용 대상이 현재 문제와 맞는가?
  4. 사실·추론·의견·가정을 구분했는가?
  5. 현재 자원과 제약에서 실제로 쓸 수 있는가?

출처 요구, 원문 대조, 반대 입장 생성, 교차질문, 복수 모델 비교, 전문가 검토를 결합한다. 여러 모델의 합의도 원문과 실행 검증을 대체하지 못한다.

Chapter 4. Hallucination and Reliability Review

Hallucination is the generation of plausible information that does not correspond to reality. Because a language model predicts likely continuations rather than consulting a guaranteed fact store, fluency and truth are separate. This is a recurring risk to manage, not a rare exception.

Failures include factual and citation errors, invalid inference, stale timing, bias, omitted context, distorted summaries, overclaiming, and missing or repeated information. A fixed review asks:

  1. Does the source exist and match the original?
  2. Is the logic from evidence to conclusion valid?
  3. Are timing and target population appropriate?
  4. Are facts, inferences, opinions, and assumptions separated?
  5. Is the recommendation usable under real constraints?

Source requests, original-document checks, opposing views, cross-questions, model comparison, and expert review work together. Model agreement cannot replace primary evidence and execution.

Practice Connection — Can We Trust AI?

Three AI systems received the same three-year post-quantum migration problem for a digital finance firm with long-lived secrets, 24/7 operations, limited budget, and an incomplete cryptographic inventory. I scored transparency, accuracy, distortion risk, interpretive range, and acceptability.

Choosing Risk-Based Staged Migration

The weighted results were 54 for immediate migration, 88 for risk-based staged migration, and 61 for minimal investment. The strategy reduced regret without pretending to predict the exact threat date.

  1. Months 0–6: inventory cryptographic assets, owners, algorithms, and data lifetimes.
  2. Months 6–18: test classical+ML-KEM hybrid paths with performance, failure, and rollback criteria.
  3. Months 18–36: migrate PKI, code signing, HSM, and SaaS contracts under validated standards and interoperability conditions.

Strategic Insight. Multiple models are useful not for majority voting but for revealing different omissions, assumptions, and operational hazards. Under uncertainty, agility, staged investment, and explicit stop/rollback rules are stronger than false precision.
